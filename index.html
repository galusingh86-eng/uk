Best Places To Visit

Kedarnath

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva.


Highlights

  • Snow Covered Mountains
  • Char Dham Temple
  • Spiritual Journey
  • Beautiful Trek

Nearby: Chopta, Tungnath

Badrinath

One of India's most sacred pilgrimage destinations.


Highlights

  • Ancient Temple
  • Mana Village
  • Neelkanth Peak
  • Hot Springs

Nearby: Mana Village

Rishikesh

Yoga Capital of the World.


Highlights

  • River Rafting
  • Bungee Jumping
  • Laxman Jhula
  • Ganga Aarti

Nearby: Haridwar

Nainital

Famous hill station with beautiful lakes.


Highlights

  • Naini Lake
  • Boating
  • Snow View Point
  • Shopping Mall Road

Nearby: Bhimtal

Auli

A famous hill station in the Chamoli district.


Highlights

  • Skiing
  • Gorson Bugyal
  • Cable Car
  • Trekking

Nearby: Valley of flowers national park

Almora

A historic, horseshoe-shaped ridge town Located in the Kumaon region .


Highlights

  • Kasar Devi Temple
  • Chitai Golu Devta Temple
  • Katarmal Sun Temple
  • Bright End Corner

Nearby: Ranikhet
